
# MacroSignal Roadmap

## M1 — Research Foundation
Typed configs, canonical domain models, explicit timing/return contracts,
research protocols, fixtures, and Git workflow.

## M2 — Point-in-Time Data
FRED/ALFRED revisions, BLS/BEA/Fed schedules, Treasury curves, availability
resolver, PIT snapshots, daily market provider.

## M3 — Ragged-Edge Macro State
Transformation registry, macro families, feature age/staleness, consensus/model
expectations, surprises/forecast errors.

## M4 — Stable Regimes & Event Research
Rule/GMM/HMM/factor regimes, state alignment, uncertainty, event-session
alignment, overlap-aware event studies.

## M5 — Signals & Portfolios
Tradable universe, return/cash/risk models, decayed macro signals, portfolio
baselines and constraints.

## M6 — Execution-Correct Backtesting
Trading calendar, walk-forward refits, availability gate, execution contract,
costs, turnover, benchmarks, regression fixtures.

## M7 — Robustness & Statistical Reporting
Dependence-aware inference, attribution, sensitivity, subperiods, model
comparison, search-bias diagnostics, reproducible report.

## M8 — Portfolio-Ready Research App
Streamlit dashboards, decision audit, CLI/demo, CI/clean-clone test, licensing
and release checks.
